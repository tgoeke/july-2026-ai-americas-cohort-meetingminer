- (technique) started Laddering
- (insight) Ladder rung 1: moment-welded artifacts = decision tracing = proof to the business — at corp people forget decisions and schedule whole meetings just to re-make the same decision
- (insight) Ladder rung 2: firm decisions -> less downstream rework — 2024 war story: absent stakeholders disagreed with UI demo outcomes months later, forcing a redesign and a 4-month schedule slip
- (idea) Absent-stakeholder review flow: review transcript + change requests fast and register agree/disagree right inside MeetingMiner — assent/dissent captured at the evidence
- (insight) Ladder rung 3: decision records are stakes in the sand -> architect designs with confidence -> new designs get vetted through the same evidence loop in future meetings -> architectures stay accurate and durable (a flywheel: MM output becomes MM input)
- (insight) Ladder top: the real need is reducing delivery chaos and stress — captured decisions on a timeline -> efficiency and profitability, but also a less toxic workplace, less exasperation, lower staff churn
- (insight) Scott/Rowan story: 'I already explained this to Rowan' — but that meeting's knowledge is inaccessible, so it must be re-explained live; meetings rehash prior meetings and everyone is exasperated
- (insight) Ladder top addendum: fewer meetings altogether — reclaiming time for actual work
