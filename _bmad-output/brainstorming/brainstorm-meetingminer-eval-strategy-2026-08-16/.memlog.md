- (idea) Scripted test decks will display ALL slides to keep ground truth clean and easy on the processor
- (idea) Over-capture sanity bound: distinct slides detected should be < meeting duration in minutes (~1 slide/min ceiling) — a guardrail metric against screenshot spam
- (idea) Dwell threshold: a screen held longer than ~20-30s should trigger a snapshot
- (idea) Design spec: watch video stream bitrate deltas — large playback changes mark candidate capture moments
