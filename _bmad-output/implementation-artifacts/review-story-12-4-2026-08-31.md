## Findings

### F-01 — The public document projection writer accepts unguarded records (high, patchable)

`projections.search.project_documents()` accepts arbitrary mappings and writes
them straight to the `documents` index through `_add()`. The only AD-18 and
AD-6 guards live in `documents.document_record()`, so a direct projection call
can persist a document with no `reviewState` / `authorship` / `reviewLabel`, or
one carrying `citable: true` / `momentId`. This defeats both code-enforced
constraints at the store boundary even though the two current production
callers happen to build their records correctly first.

Remediation: make the public writer validate every supplied record before the
first Meilisearch operation, and add red-first tests proving that an unlabelled
record and a citation-shaped record are refused without touching the client.
